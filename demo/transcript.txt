$ fragility explain
fragility measures four fragility modes of AI-written code:

  tests          shallow tests on the changed lines
  abstraction    over-complex / over-DRY code, hard to debug
  comprehension  lines nobody understands or re-touched
  review         PRs arriving faster than human review
  score          how the composite FRAGILITY SCORE is built

Run `fragility explain <signal>` for the measurement method,
thresholds, and sources of one signal.
